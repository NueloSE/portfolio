Hindsight

An AI post-trade coach for tokenized US stocks

Replays every trade against real market data, finds the habits that cost the trader money, proves each one with their own trades, and turns them into rules that check the next one.

  • "Code calculates, the AI explains". Every number comes from tested code, not the LLM

Formwork

A visa application you and your AI agent fill in together

A 26-field form that works normally by hand, but also exposes safe tools to a browser agent through WebMCP. The agent can read your documents, fill fields visibly and flag doubts, but it can never submit.

  • 7 imperative tools plus 1 declarative tool, all sharing the same handlers the UI buttons use
  • Built for screen-reader and motor-impaired users, with no backend, keys or secrets

Ajora

Trustless rotating savings (ajo / esusu) on Stellar

Contribution societies fund small businesses across West Africa, until an organiser disappears with the pot. Ajora replaces the organiser with a smart contract that enforces contributions and pays out automatically.
